1. planets are considered to influence another planet when within 5°.
2. in this system of interpretation each house has a Most Effective Point (MEP) the MEP of each house is the rising degree of the chart (found in the Lagna)
3. Based on point 2. a planet can influence a house if it is within 5° of the MEP.
4. planets also cast aspects into other houses. Every planet casts a 7th house aspect. Aspects are counted starting with the position of the planet and counting counterclockwise around the chart. A 7th house aspect is 180° from the position of the planet. and is arrived at by counting 1 at the current position and so around the chart to the count of 7 which arrives at the house exactly opposite the starting position. So... starting at 1 not 0.
5. aspects have the same influence as the planet itself. For instance a 7th house aspect within 5° of a planet in that opposite house will be influenced as if it were within 5°.
6. for example in the test chart "Paul" Mars in the 1st house has a 4th house aspect to Jupiter in the 4th house aspect is 100% overlap.
7. for example in the test chart "Paul" Venus is conjunct (aspects at count of 1) by Mercury and the reverse. They overlap at <1° close conjunction of two benefic planets.
8. for example in the test chart "Paul" both Venus and Mars are conjunct / overlap the 5° around the MEP of the 11th house. Since both planets are benefic they enhance the qualities of the house and Mercury and Venus both cast an aspect into the 5th house. This strengthens the house and since it is the root/moolatrikona sign of the Jupiter also strengthens that planet.
9. the degree of overlap increases the effect: good for benefic planets and bad malefics.
