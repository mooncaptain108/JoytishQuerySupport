For each rising sign there is a distinct set of malefic planets.

Besides Rahu and Ketu, following are the functional malefic planets for various ascendants:
each sign has a most malefic planet - (MMP)


Ascendant                      Functional Malefics
--------------------------------------------------
1.Aries (Mesh)                 Mercury (MMP=Ketu)
2.Taurus (Vrishbha)            Mars, Venus and Jupiter(MMP)
3.Gemini (Mithuna)             None (MMP=Ketu)
4.Cancer (Karkata)             Jupiter and Saturn(MMP)
5.Leo (Simha)                  The Moon (Moon)
6.Virgo (Kanya)                The Sun, Saturn and Mars(MMP)
7.Libra (Tula)                 Mercury(MMP)
8.Scorpio (Vrishchak)          Venus(MMP) and Mars
9.Sagittarius (Dhanu)          The Moon(MMP)
10.Capricorn (Makar)           The Sun(MMP) and Jupiter
11.Aquarius (Kumbh)            The Moon and Mercury(MMP)
12.Pisces (Mina)               The Sun, Venus(MMP) and Saturn
