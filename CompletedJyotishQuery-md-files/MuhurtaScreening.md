

Afflicted  means within 5°.

Search only in Aires (lagna lord Mars), Gemini (lagna lord Sun), Leo (lagna lord Sun), Libra (lagna lord Venus), Sagittarius (lagna lord Jupiter)

Dismiss if any of the following are afflicted:
   1. MEP of lagna lord house.
   2. MEP of occupied house of lagna lord.
   3. lagna lord.

Dismiss if there are any badly placed planets.
Dismiss if the starting antardasha planet is not a well placed, strong benefic planet.

Dismiss if there is a concentration of malefic influence.
Any of the following qualifies as a concentration:
    1. Any special affliction.
    2. More than one affliction to the MEP of an MT sign.
    3. One affliction to the MEP of an MT sign along with a separate affliction to the MT lord. (this may be new)

To qualify after the above tests are passed.
    1. The Moon (Malefic or Benefic) is in the Nakshatra of a benefic planet.
    2. Two or more strong planets. Not counting Rahu or Ketu but could include Mercury and the Moon if either is Malefic. 
    3. Five or more strong houses.
