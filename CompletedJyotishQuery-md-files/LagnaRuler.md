When the first house doesn't contain an MT sign the rulership goes to the 3rd house lord if 3rd house doesn't contain an MT sign then 2nd house Lord rules the Lagna.

The only time this matters in this program's logic is when screening for Muhurtas. Afflictions to this planet or to its MT sign of any kind disqualifies the chart. Same for all the other lords of charts.
