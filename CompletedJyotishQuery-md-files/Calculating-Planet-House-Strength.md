LOSS OF STRENGTH BY PLANETS

Affliction calculation: When phrase "Extent of n%" appears below. use (n *  (1-(orb/5)))

- a) A planet will lose strength to the extent of 75% if its mooltrikona sign house is afflicted. 
- b) A weak planet placed in an afflicted house will lose strength to the extent of 75%.
- c) An otherwise strong planet placed in a non-mooltrikona sign afflicted house will lose strength to the extent of 50%.
- d) A closely afflicted weak planet will lose strength to the extent of 75%.
- e) A closely afflicted otherwise strong planet will lose strength to the extent of 50%.
- f1) A planet becoming weak due to close combustion will lose strength to the extent of 75% if the Sun is a - functional malefic planet. 
- f2) Where the Sun is a functional benefic and it causes combustion to another planet, the planet will become 50% - weak
- f3) A combust planet in its sign of debilitation and placed in a malefic house will have only 10% power.
- g)  When planets are placed in the malefic houses, they generally lose strength by 50%.
- h1) When planets are placed in their signs of debilitation, they lose strength by 50%.
- h2) When planets are placed in their signs of debilitation in both the birth chart and navamsa the D9 chart, will - lose strength by 75%.
- i) If in the Rasi (main) chart the planet is badly placed and at the same time debilitated in navamsa it would lose - strength by 60%.
- j) A badly placed planet in its sign of debilitation will lose its power by 75%.
- k) A planet debilitated in the navamsa D9 chart will lose power by 25%.

GAIN OF STRENGTH BY PLANETS THROUGH ASPECTS AND CONJUNCTIONS:

- Aspected or conjunct from strong benefic planet(>= 70% strength). The strength is raised by (50% * (1-(orb/5))) where orb of 0 degrees is exact and orb of 5 degrees yields no improvement. Do not apply if orb is > 5°
- An exact conjunction or aspect by a weak functional benefic planet (< 70%) raises the strength of the planet by 12.5%. This benefit is only applicable for exact conjunction or aspect. For practical application say <1° is exact. (12.5% * (1-(orb))) do not apply unless orb is < 1°.

GAIN OF STRENGTH FOR HOUSES

The strength of Mooltrikona sign houses is the same strength as their lord.
The strength of non-Mooltrikona houses can be influenced by malefic or benefic planets. If no planet is conjunct or aspects the MEP of a non-Mooltrikona sign then consider that house to be 100% strong. A non-Mooltrikona house can have its strength increased by aspect of benefic planet up to 50%. add (50% * (1-(orb/5))) to strength.
