OWN SIGN:  A planet in its own sign is treated as strong and is capable of generating the expected results, provided it is otherwise strong.

For the purposes of evaluating dispositor status and if an affliction or benefic influence to an MEP benefits a planet ignore OWN signs. Only mooltrikona/root signs can have a dispositor or convey impacts of mep to the ruling planet.

The own signs of the planets are as follows:
SUN- LEO (Leo is both an Own and Root sign)
MOON- CANCER (cancer is both an Own and Root sign)
MARS- ARIES(also root) SCORPIO
MERCURY- GEMINI, VIRGO (also root)
JUPITER- SAGITTARIUS (also root), PISCES
VENUS- TAURUS, LIBRA (also root)
SATURN- CAPRICORN, AQUARIUS (also root)

ROOT / MOOLTRIKONA SIGNS : A planet in its mooltrikona (MT) sign is treated as very powerful, provided it is otherwise strong.

SUN- LEO
MOON- CANCER
MARS- ARIES
MERCURY- VIRGO
JUPITER- SAGITTARIUS
VENUS- LIBRA
SATURN- AQUARIUS

Only root signs can have a dispositor.
